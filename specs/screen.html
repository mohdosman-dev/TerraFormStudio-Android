Elena Rossi
Master Artisan Tuscany, Italy
Inspiration drawn from rolling sienna hills and the rhythmic breath of the Tyrrhenian Sea.

Elena Rossi’s work is a dialogue between the raw earth of Tuscany and the disciplined hands of a lifelong maker.

Her process is slow and attentive, with local stoneware, mineral glazes, and firing methods that ensure each piece carries its own atmosphere.

The Material
Local Tuscan stoneware, filtered mountain water, and hand-ground mineral oxides.
The Firing
Oxidation firing at 1220°C for strength and restrained tonal depth.
The Glaze
Signature matte Oatmeal and Dusty Sage glazes refined in studio over a decade.
